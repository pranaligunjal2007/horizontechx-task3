# Horizon Techx Task 3 – Inc. 5000 Company Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of my **Artificial Intelligence & Data Science internship at Horizon Tech**.

The project focuses on analyzing the **Inc. 5000 Company dataset** using Python and Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) techniques. The aim is to clean the dataset, explore company performance, identify trends and patterns, and generate meaningful business insights.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Clean and preprocess the dataset
- Handle missing values and duplicate records
- Perform statistical analysis
- Analyze company industries and locations
- Study revenue and company growth
- Identify top-performing companies
- Analyze relationships between numerical variables
- Create meaningful data visualizations
- Generate business insights from the analysis

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains information about approximately **5,000 companies** from the Inc. 5000 list.

Important columns include:

- Company
- Rank
- Workers
- Revenue
- Growth
- Industry
- State
- City
- Metro
- Years on List

## 📈 Analysis Performed

The project includes:

- Industry-wise company distribution
- State-wise company distribution
- Revenue distribution
- Top 10 companies by revenue
- Top 10 fastest-growing companies
- Correlation analysis
- Revenue by industry
- Average growth by industry
- Relationship between workers and revenue


## 💡 Key Insights

- The dataset contains approximately 5,000 companies from different industries.
- Company revenue varies considerably, indicating differences in business scale.
- Industry analysis shows that some sectors contain a larger number of companies than others.
- Growth analysis helps identify companies and industries with strong growth potential.
- State-wise analysis shows the geographical distribution of companies.
- The workers-versus-revenue analysis helps examine the relationship between company size and revenue.
- Correlation analysis provides insights into relationships among rank, workers, growth, revenue, and years on the list.
